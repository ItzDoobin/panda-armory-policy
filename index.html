Privacy Policy for Panda Armory and Doob Games

Updated November 18, 2026

Panda Armory and Doob Games are Twitch extensions operated for the ItzDoobin channel. They share a DoobCoin wallet and panda wardrobe. This notice explains the information used by the extensions and their connected channel tools.

Information used

When you allow Twitch identity sharing, the extensions use your numeric Twitch user ID and available Twitch username or display name. The service associates that identity with your virtual DoobCoin balance, owned items, equipped outfit, and daily reward timestamp. Doob Games also stores game choices, stakes, moves, results, session state, achievements, and recovery records. Private game state, such as cards or mines, is processed to run the games.

The service also stores transaction records, including request identifiers, purchases, sales, rewards, balance changes, and completion times. Connected channel tools may record the relevant Twitch message or redemption identifier, participant identities, and game or reward state to process an action and prevent duplicate payments.

Why it is used

This information authenticates your account, loads and saves your inventory, processes virtual-currency transactions, recovers interrupted requests, prevents repeated charges or rewards, and supports channel games and rewards. Your username, equipped panda, and reward results may appear in the broadcaster's on-stream overlays when you use those features. Leaderboards display names or pseudonyms, ranks, and virtual coin totals. Multiplayer views may display player identifiers, names, outfits, actions, stakes, and game results to other participants. Account-specific history is restricted by the application's account checks.

Online storage and service providers

Account data and transaction records are stored online in the broadcaster's Supabase project. The extension frontend and its images are hosted on Twitch. When Streamer.bot is connected, it keeps local display copies of profiles and outfits and local pending-request files so stream actions can recover after a connection interruption.

Twitch and Supabase process information as platform and hosting providers. This means the service is not limited to data stored locally on the broadcaster's computer. We do not sell, rent, or lease your information or use it to track you across unrelated websites. Twitch and Supabase also publish their own privacy information.

Browser storage

Doob Games uses browser local storage for favorites and recent games without an application-set expiry. Both extensions use session storage for pending actions; Doob Games may also retain failed-action recovery evidence there. Clearing browser storage removes those local records but does not delete server-side account records.

Hosting and platform providers may process technical logs under their own privacy practices. This notice does not imply that provider logs exclude IP addresses or share the application's retention period.

Retention and identity sharing

Balances, inventory, and transaction history persist between sessions. Transaction records are retained to support recovery, accounting for virtual coins, and duplicate prevention; they are not automatically erased when you close the extension. You can revoke Twitch identity sharing through Twitch, which prevents the extension from accessing your linked profile through that permission. Revoking permission does not automatically delete previously stored records.

Questions and deletion requests

Contact itzdoobin@gmail.com with privacy questions or to request deletion of your Panda Armory or Doob Games data. Include your Twitch username so the operator can identify the account and verify that the request concerns your data. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other credentials. Deletion requests are handled by the operator rather than an automatic in-extension control.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the extension or its data handling changes. The update date will appear at the top of this page.
