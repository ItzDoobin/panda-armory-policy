Privacy Policy for Panda Armory

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Panda Armory ("we," "our," or "us") provides this Twitch extension to enhance your viewing experience. Because this extension utilizes the Twitch Request Identity Link feature, we are committed to being fully transparent about how your data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you grant permission via the Twitch Identity Link, this extension accesses your numeric Twitch User ID and Twitch Username.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected Twitch User ID and Username are used solely to provide core extension functionality, including:

  • Saving and loading your virtual inventory, currency balance, and equipped stream items.
  • Authenticating your profile within the localized extension framework.

This data is strictly localized to the broadcaster's stream and is not shared with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or lease your data. Your information is never used for tracking across unrelated applications or websites.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Any data processed through the Twitch Identity Link is retained only for as long as necessary to maintain your active session, inventory settings, or stream engagement logs. You can revoke this permission at any time directly through your Twitch account connections panel.

4. Data Deletion Requests

To request the absolute deletion of your stored localized data, please contact the broadcaster directly.

5. Updates to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our extension configuration or platform policies. Any updates will be reflected by a revised effective date.
